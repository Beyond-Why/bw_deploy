There are actually **two different questions hiding here.**

Why is the electron *negative*?

That part is convention. Benjamin Franklin could have named the electron's kind of charge “positive” and the proton's “negative.” Nothing in physics would have changed.

But: **Why does the electron have electric charge at all?**

That's a much deeper question.

The electron is an elementary particle, and its electromagnetic charge is built into how it participates in the Standard Model's gauge symmetry. Physics tells us remarkably precisely **how** that charge behaves, but doesn't currently derive the electron's charge from something more fundamental.

So the minus sign is our label.

**The charge itself is not.**
