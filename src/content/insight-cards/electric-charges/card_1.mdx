**Charge is what makes matter “electrically visible.”**

Give an electron a charge of −e, and a proton +e. Put either one in empty space and it changes the electromagnetic field around it. 
Put another charge there, and that field produces a force on it.

The rule is captured by Coulomb's law:

$$
F = k \dfrac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

> Like charges push apart. Opposite charges pull together.

So charge isn't a tiny substance stored inside an electron.<br />
**It is a property that determines how a particle creates and responds to the electromagnetic field.**
