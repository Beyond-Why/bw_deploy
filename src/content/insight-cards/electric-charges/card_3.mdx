You can create a charged object, but you don't create charge from nothing.

Rub a balloon against your hair and electrons move from one material to the other. The balloon becomes negative; your hair becomes positive. The charges were separated, not created.

The rule is:
$$
Q_{\text{total}} = \text{constant}
$$
Even particle reactions obey it. An electron (−e) and positron (+e) can disappear together, but their total charge was zero before and remains zero afterward.

**Charge can move, split between objects, and even disappear with its opposite. But the total charge never loses the ledger.**
