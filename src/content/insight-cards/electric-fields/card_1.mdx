You already know that a charge can push or pull another charge. But saying “the two charges exert a force on each other” leaves a strange question: **how does one charge know the other is there?**

The electric field is the answer.<br />A charge changes the electromagnetic condition of the space around it. At every point, we can describe that field by asking:

> If I placed a tiny positive test charge here, which way would it be pushed, and how strongly?

That gives us:
$$
\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}
$$

The field exists whether or not we actually put the test charge there.
