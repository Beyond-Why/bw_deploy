Because creating an electric field isn't an additional thing a charge does. **It is part of what having electric charge means.**

An electric charge is coupled to the electromagnetic field. Wherever the charge exists, its presence determines the field around it.

For a point charge:
$$
E = k\frac{|q|}{r^2}
$$

Double the charge, and the field doubles. Move twice as far away, and the field becomes four times weaker. <br />This is why the field is not merely a convenient drawing. **It is measurable physical quantity that determines what force another charge would experience.**
