A field isn't just "how much force is around here". It also tells you **which way the force would act.**

By convention, the direction of an electric field is the direction a **positive test charge** would be pushed. <br />So:

- Around a positive charge, the field points outward.
- Around a negative charge, it points inward.

Since fields have direction, they can be combined at any point. The total field is the **vector sum** of the fields at that point.
