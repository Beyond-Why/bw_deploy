# The Field That Powers The World

Picture electricity as water through pipes. Electrons drifting through copper, pushed from the battery, rushing toward the bulb. 
<br />It's the mental picture most of us carry around. 
<br /><br /><b>But here's the problem.</b> 
<br /><br />Electrons in a wire drift at about 0.1 millimeters per second. Slower than a crawling ant. 
<br />Yet the moment you flip a switch, the bulb on the far side of the house already knows what to do. 
<br /><br /><b>So what actually moved?</b>
