Meet the robot. It's about the size of a shoebox lid, and everything it knows about the world comes through four parts.

**Eyes.** Five light sensors in a row under its nose. Each one shines a tiny infrared light at the floor and measures how much bounces back. White mat bounces a lot. Black tape bounces almost nothing.

**Hands.** A two-finger gripper on the front, driven by one small servo. It opens, it closes, and that's the whole repertoire.

**Legs.** Two wheels, each with its own motor, and a little ball at the back so it doesn't tip over. It steers by running one wheel faster than the other.

**Brain.** A small board that reads the five sensors a few hundred times a second and decides how hard to push each wheel. That decision is what this whole log is about.
