The kit came today. Forty-one parts, one diagram, and a strong smell of new plastic.

I put the chassis together in about two hours. The wheels went on backwards the first time, which I only noticed when I told it to go forward and it went politely backward instead.

The task, as the competition puts it: follow a black line around a mat, pick up a block at one stop, drop it at another. Sounds like a weekend. I'm writing this down so future me can laugh at that sentence.
