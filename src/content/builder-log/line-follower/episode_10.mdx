The gripper works without me pressing anything. The robot drives up to the block, stops when the front sensor sees it, and closes its two fingers.

The first few tries it closed too early and hugged thin air. Moving the stop point two centimetres closer fixed it.

It can't carry the block anywhere yet. It just holds it, very proudly, and waits.
