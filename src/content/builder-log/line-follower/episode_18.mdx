I printed the real practice mat and laid it out in the hall. Same code, same robot, and it can't find the line at all.

It turns out the hall has a big window. In the afternoon the sunlight is strong enough that the sensors see "bright" everywhere, black tape included.

I closed the curtains and it worked again. That's not a fix, that's hiding. Tomorrow I find out what the sensors are actually seeing.
