One metre of black tape on the kitchen floor, and it stayed on it the whole way.

The rule is as simple as I could make it: if the left sensor sees black, turn left. If the right one does, turn right. Otherwise go straight. It zig-zags like it's had too much coffee, but it never left the tape.

Then it reached the end of the tape and drove confidently under the fridge. One problem at a time.
